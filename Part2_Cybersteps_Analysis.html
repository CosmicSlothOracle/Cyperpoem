"165.232.131.154" | naabu -p - -silent -rate 2000 -retries 2

Why these specific rate limits?

  • naabu: -rate 2000 caps packets/sec; -retries 2 avoids repeated probes.
  • nmap: -T2/T3 (slower timing), --max-retries 2, and bounded RTT timeouts (--max-rtt-timeout 3s) ensure probes don't hang.

Reasoning: A port guarded by a firewall drops the packet. A stateless filter blocks the reply. Some IDS/IPS silently drop it. There is no reconnaissance value gained in waiting for an RST or a reply.
